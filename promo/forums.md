**Title:** [Free tool] DualSense adaptive triggers & haptics for Forza Horizon 6 on PC

**Body:**

The PC version doesn't use the DualSense adaptive triggers or haptics, so I made a small free tool that does it from the game's Data Out telemetry.

- L2 feels like a brake pedal with a bite point, ABS pulses and a "wall" right before the wheels lock
- R2: wheelspin, rev limiter, gear-shift kick
- Haptics: engine character per car, weight transfer in corners, crashes from the correct side, jump landings

Setup: turn on *Settings → HUD and Gameplay → Data Out*, IP 127.0.0.1, port 5300, run the exe, choose direct USB (free ViGEmBus + HidHide) or DSX.

Open source, ~100 KB, no install: https://github.com/GefryV3/ForzaTriggers-DualSense-Forza-Horizon
