# Demo video — script & shot list

The hard part of selling "you can feel it" is that viewers can't feel it. So the video shows **cause → trigger/controller reaction** side by side: game footage + a picture-in-picture of the hand on the controller + the app's live panel.

## Gear
- Phone on a clamp above the controller (top-down, hand visible, triggers visible from the side if possible).
- OBS capturing the game; app window on a second monitor (captured too).
- Optional: contact mic or phone mic taped near the controller — the haptics are audible and that sells it.
